What kind of transaction?

ExpenseSpending from an account or credit card IncomeMoney received, such as salary or a cash rebate TransferMove money between your balances, or pay off a card Asset PurchaseBuy something you keep as an asset, like a laptop Return on InvestmentDividends, interest or other investment returns Asset SaleSell all or part of an asset, at a gain or a loss Repay DebtPay down a credit card or loan from an account

Add a transaction

Entire asset sale Fills in the principal with the asset’s current amount (you can still change it). The asset is hidden from your balances after saving; it can be shown again from Balances.
Interest and fees (optional)
Paid from the account on top of the principal; only the principal lowers the debt. Counted as expenses in reports.
Gain / loss (optional) Positive for a gain, negative for a loss. The asset goes down by the principal; the account receives the principal plus the gain (or minus the loss). Counted as income or expense in reports.
Foreign currency amount (optional)
What it cost or paid in another currency, e.g. a USD purchase charged to a THB card. For reference only: balances and totals use the amount above.
Date and time

Saving updates the selected balance(s) automatically: an expense lowers an account’s balance or raises a card’s debt, income raises a balance, a transfer moves the amount from source to destination, an asset purchase pays like an expense and raises the asset’s value, an asset sale lowers the asset’s value by the principal and credits the account with the principal plus the gain (or minus the loss), a debt repayment lowers the account’s balance by the principal plus any interest and fees, and the card’s or loan’s debt by the principal. The currency must match the (source) balance’s currency; a transfer between balances in different currencies credits the received amount, converted at the ECB reference rate from Frankfurter unless you type it yourself. Editing a balance adjustment or beginning balance moves its balance by the difference.

Totals per currency

Delete balance?

Hiding keeps the balance and its transactions (they still count in totals and reports) and only takes it off this list and the transaction form. You can show it again later.
